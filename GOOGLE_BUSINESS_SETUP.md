# Google Business Profile Setup Guide
## Putovný mužský kruh - Blansko & Brno

### Overview
Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) will help your men's circle appear in Google Maps and local search results when people search for:
- "mužský kruh Blansko"
- "stretnutia mužov Brno"
- "mužská podpora Blanensko"

### Step-by-Step Setup

#### 1. Create Google Business Profile
**URL:** https://www.google.com/business/

**Account Requirements:**
- Gmail account associated with the organization
- Phone number for verification

#### 2. Choose Business Category

**Primary Category:** Community Organization
**Additional Categories:**
- Non-Profit Organization
- Social Services Organization
- Support Group

**Why these categories:** They accurately represent a men's support circle while being recognized by Google.

#### 3. Service Area Configuration

Since Putovný kruh is a traveling circle without a fixed address:

**Business Type:** Select "I deliver goods and services to my customers"

**Service Areas:**
- Blansko
- Brno
- Adamov
- Boskovice
- Letovice
- Rájec-Jestřebí
- Sloup
- Křtiny
- Ráječko
- Surrounding Blanensko region

**Geographic Coordinates (for schema/maps):**
- Latitude: 49.3626
- Longitude: 16.6441
(This is Blansko's center - adjust if needed)

Since meetings are scheduled events, not regular business hours:

**Option A - Mark as "By Appointment Only"**

#### 6. Photos (REQUIRED for local SEO)

Google Business Profiles with photos get **42% more requests for directions** and **35% more click-throughs**.

**Required Photos:**
1. **Logo** (minimum 250x250px, recommended 1000x1000px)
   - Upload: `/public/images/logo.jpg`

2. **Cover Photo** (minimum 480x270px, recommended 1920x1080px)
   - Fire circle gathering (image04.jpg could work)
   - Should show the atmosphere of your meetings

3. **Additional Photos (3-5 recommended):**
   - Fire at night (if you have one)
   - Speaking object
   - Nature locations where you meet
   - Men sitting in circle (if you have permission)

**Photo Guidelines:**
- JPG or PNG format
- File size: Between 10KB - 5MB
- Resolution: Minimum 720px tall/wide
- Don't add logos/watermarks (Google penalizes this)

#### 10. Reviews Management

**How to get reviews:**
1. After each circle, send follow-up message via WhatsApp
2. Ask participants to share their experience on Google
3. Provide direct link: `https://g.page/r/[your-business-id]/review`

**Responding to reviews:**
- Thank every reviewer within 24-48 hours
- Keep responses authentic and personal
- Address any concerns professionally

#### 11. Verification Process

Google will verify your business via:
- **Phone call** (most common)
- **Postcard** (if physical address provided)
- **Email** (rarely available)
- **Video verification** (for service-area businesses)

**Video Verification Tips:**
- Show yourself in a location you meet (forest, fire circle spot)
- Hold up any materials/signs with business name
- Explain the service you provide

#### 12. Local SEO Optimization

**After setup, regularly:**
- Post weekly updates (2-3x per week)
- Upload new photos from meetings (monthly)
- Respond to all reviews within 48 hours
- Update business hours if schedule changes
- Add new services/offerings as they develop

#### 13. Insights & Analytics

Monitor your Google Business Profile performance:
- **Search queries:** See what terms people use to find you
- **Actions:** Track website clicks, direction requests, phone calls
- **Views:** Monitor profile views over time

**Check monthly and adjust strategy based on:**
- Which keywords drive the most views
- Which posts get the most engagement
- Geographic distribution of viewers
